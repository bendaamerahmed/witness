# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Criticize the code, the claim, or the number — never the person.

## Specifics

**Expected:** clear technical disagreement, evidence for assertions, graceful acceptance of being wrong, and patience with people who are new to this.

**Not accepted:** harassment of any kind, personal attacks, demeaning or discriminatory comments, publishing others' private information, or sustained disruption.

## A note particular to this project

This repository is about honesty in measurement. Two things follow.

Reporting that a number here is wrong is a contribution, not an attack — including when the number is one the maintainers published. Say so plainly and show your work; it will be taken seriously.

And overstating a result is a conduct issue here, not merely a technical one. Quoting the benchmark without its control column, or a precision figure without saying the corpus is hand-written, misleads people who trust this project. Don't.

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues to the maintainer via a [private security advisory](https://github.com/bendaamerahmed/witness/security/advisories/new) or a direct message. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or ban accounts for behaviour they judge inappropriate.
